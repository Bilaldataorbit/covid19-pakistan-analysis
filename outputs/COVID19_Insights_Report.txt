READING NOTES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * CFR and recovery rate are cumulative ratios; they are unstable in the
    first weeks when the denominator is tiny.
  * Recovered is under-reported, so the recovery rate is a lower bound.
  * Exposure source and province are correlated, so provincial CFR
    differences are not a clean measure of care quality.
  * Forecasts are scenario envelopes, not predictions.
